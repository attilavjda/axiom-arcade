World Hopper: truth and possible worlds

A modal logic game. A sentence falls toward a row of worlds. Say whether it is true or false at the world alpha, where you stand. Z or left arrow: true. X or right arrow: false. In the Valid or Not level, Z or left arrow: valid, X or right arrow: not valid. In the Break It level, keys 1 to 6 switch lamps, Enter checks, V claims valid. On the title screen, space opens the level menu: up and down arrows choose, space plays, keys 1 to 9 jump straight to a level. Escape: back to the level menu. H: rules. O: accessibility options. S: timer normal, slow or off. C: high contrast. R: calm mode without flashing or blinking. L: read aloud. A: repeat the last announcement. P: pause. M: sound. You have unlimited hearts, so the game never ends.

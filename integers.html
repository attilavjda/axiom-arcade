Zero to Z: numbers and arithmetic in lambda-D

A game about chapter 14 of Type Theory and Formal Proof: the integers built from zero, the successor s and the predecessor p. Space on the title screen opens the level menu: up and down arrows choose, space plays, keys 1 to 7 jump to a level. Levels 1 and 2: left and right arrows walk, space stops. Level 3: left and right arrows pick a point, space marks it, enter checks; then Z or X judges the axiom. Level 4: arrows move, Z: breaks going up, X: breaks going down, B: P of 0 fails, V: P holds everywhere. Levels 5 and 6: keys 1 to 5 apply a rule. Level 7: Z true, X false. Escape: level menu. H: rules. O: accessibility. C: high contrast. R: calm mode. L: read aloud. A: repeat. P: pause. M: sound. There is no timer and no game over.
